Paid overnight sites — Connellsville to Evitts Creek

93.8 trail miles with no free campsites: GAP mile 89.4 (Connellsville) to GAP mile 0 (Cumberland), then C&O mile 184.5 to 180.1 (Evitts Creek). These are the paid places in that stretch, Pittsburgh → Cumberland → Evitts Creek. Prices are per night and change — verify before a trip, especially the ones marked “about”.

Cheapest verified-current campground night here is Outflow tent at $25. The C&O side of the gap (Cumberland to Evitts Creek, 4.4 miles) has no paid site at all — the only option at the junction is the Cumberland YMCA. No Canal Quarters lockhouse is in this section either; the nearest is Lockhouse 49 at C&O mile 108.9, about 71 miles south of Evitts Creek.
