Virginia Campsites

123 campgrounds across Virginia: the George Washington & Jefferson National Forests, National Park Service sites, U.S. Army Corps of Engineers lakes, and Virginia State Parks. See also the Pennsylvania and West Virginia campsites pages.

Sites marked Free are confirmed free: four national forest sites and two Park Service backcountry sites. State park fees are shown as resident/nonresident pairs from the DCR schedule effective May 12, 2025; a $5 transaction fee and Virginia sales tax are added to all state park fees. Campgrounds with no fee shown have unverified rates — check the linked page. Many state park pins are park-level or approximate — see the site notes. Fees are 2026-season figures and may change.

Data compiled Nov 15, 2026 from Recreation.gov / RIDB, Forest Service, Park Service, Corps, and Virginia DCR sources. Sites with no link have no confirmed listing page. Verify status and fees before a trip.

Notes: camping is prohibited on all 26 Virginia state forests except by special manager-issued permit for organized groups; undesignated dispersed camping in the national forests follows forest orders (14 days in any 30, no camping within 300 ft of developed parking areas or boat ramps); Shenandoah backcountry camping is by Recreation.gov permit only, no fixed sites; there is no camping in the Virginia district of Assateague Island. False Cape State Park has 12 primitive tent sites across 4 hike/boat-in areas ($21/$27, reservations required) with no confirmed pins, and the Laurel Fork Primitive Camp at North Fork of Pound Lake (boat/foot access only) has no confirmed location or fee — neither is pinned. Closed campgrounds are left off this page.
