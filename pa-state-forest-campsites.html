Pennsylvania Campsites

828 campsites across Pennsylvania: 801 designated sites on state forest land, from the DCNR GIS layer, plus 26 free primitive sites and Loleta Campground in the Allegheny National Forest (Clarion River & Millstone Creek). Full Clarion detail: see the Clarion River & Millstone Creek page.

Most state forest sites need a paid permit/reservation — the site name links to its reservation page, with fees and availability there. The DCNR data has no fee field, so no state forest site here is labeled free. The 26 Clarion/Millstone sites are marked Free — confirmed free, first-come, first-served, no permit. Permit status is shown as in the data; if a site’s description disagrees, check with the district office.

Coordinates are DCNR’s published values. Sites with no reservation link are not in the reservation system — contact the district office. Data pulled Nov 15, 2026.

Blue dots: motorized. Green: primitive. Teal: water trail. Amber: trail shelter. Purple: camping area. Tap a dot for details.
