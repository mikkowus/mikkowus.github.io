GAP + C&O Canal — Free Hiker-Biker Campsites

35 free, first-come-first-served hiker-biker campsites on the Pittsburgh to Georgetown ride: 4 on the Great Allegheny Passage (Pittsburgh to Cumberland) and 31 on the C&O Canal towpath (Cumberland to Georgetown). One night per site, no fee, no reservation. C&O sites have a chemical toilet, picnic table, grill, and non-potable well water (mid-April to mid-November — filter, treat, or boil); GAP sites have pit/composting toilets, picnic tables, fire rings, and potable water in season (April 15–October 31). Distances are trail miles from the previous site (milepost difference) — the distance you actually ride/hike, not straight-line distance. Note the mile flip at Cumberland: GAP mile 0 = C&O mile 184.5.

Count note: NPS says 31 in some places and 30 in its 2026 Superintendent’s Compendium, with no site list in the latter. All 31 sites below are distinct published sites, so all 31 are listed here.

Great Allegheny Passage — free sites (Pittsburgh to Cumberland)

Only 4 free sites on the whole 150-mile GAP, all between GAP miles 89.4 and 122.6. From Connellsville (89.4) to Cumberland (mile 0) — 89.4 trail miles — there are no further free sites; every overnight option there is paid. GAP miles run Pittsburgh → Cumberland, so the first free site from Pittsburgh is Dravo, ~27.4 miles from the Point. Free, first-come-first-served, one-night stays; potable water in most cases April 15–October 31. Roundbottom is listed May–September, the other three year-round.

Longest gap on the whole Pittsburgh → Georgetown ride: Connellsville (GAP 89.4) to Evitts Creek (C&O 180.1) is 93.8 trail miles — 89.4 miles to Cumberland, then 4.4 miles down the C&O. No free sites in between.
