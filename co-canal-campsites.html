C&O Canal — Free Hiker-Biker Campsites

31 free, first-come-first-served hiker-biker campsites along the 184.5-mile C&O Canal towpath, Georgetown to Cumberland, MD. One night per site. Each has a chemical toilet, picnic table, grill, and non-potable well water (mid-April to mid-November — filter, treat, or boil). No fee, no reservation. Distances are towpath miles from the previous site (milepost difference) — the distance you actually ride/hike, not straight-line distance.

Count note: NPS says 31 in some places and 30 in its 2026 Superintendent’s Compendium, with no site list in the latter. All 31 sites below are distinct published sites, so all 31 are listed here.
