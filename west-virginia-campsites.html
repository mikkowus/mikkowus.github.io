West Virginia Campsites

93 campgrounds across West Virginia: U.S. Army Corps of Engineers lakes, the Monongahela and George Washington National Forests, National Park Service sites, WV State Parks, and WV State Forests. See also the Pennsylvania campsites page.

Sites marked Free are confirmed free: the National Park Service campgrounds, Camp Run, and three Monongahela dispersed camping areas. Developed campgrounds are marked with their nightly fee where a rate could be verified; campgrounds with no fee shown have unverified rates — check the linked page. Fees are as published and may change. Many state park and state forest pins are park-level or approximate — see the site notes.

Data compiled Nov 15, 2026 from Recreation.gov / RIDB, Forest Service, Park Service, Corps, and WV State Parks sources. Sites with no link have no confirmed listing page. Verify status and fees before a trip.

Blue dots: campgrounds. Green: primitive. Amber: group camps. Tap a dot for details.
