Maryland Campsites

154 campgrounds across Maryland: National Park Service sites, the Army Corps campground at Youghiogheny River Lake, Navy and Army recreation areas, Maryland State Parks, Maryland State Forests, and DNR natural resources management areas. See also the Pennsylvania, West Virginia, and Virginia campsites pages.

No government campground in Maryland was verified free — every confirmed site charges a fee, so there is no free-sites filter on this page. State park and forest fees are base rates: add $1/night for non-residents, $1/night on weekends and holidays, and a $6.25 transaction fee per stay. Youth group sites show $0 with a Youth Group Pass; reservation/service fees may still apply, and they are not general free camping. Green Ridge's numbered sites are pinned from DNR's driving-tour map (positions estimated, within a few hundred yards); Savage River publishes only area maps, so its row is an area entry with a headquarters pin — see the notes. The 31 free C&O Canal hiker-biker sites are already on the C&O Canal page and are not duplicated here.

Data compiled Nov 15–17, 2026 from Recreation.gov / RIDB, National Park Service, Navy, Corps, and Maryland DNR sources. Sites with no link have no confirmed listing page. Verify status and fees before a trip.
